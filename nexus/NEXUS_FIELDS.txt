MOD NAME
Mutagen - Save Editor

SUMMARY (brief overview, under 350 characters)
Save editor for TMNT: Splintered Fate. Change Dragon/Dreamer coins and materials, swap your hero mid-run (drag and drop), add or level powers, max upgrades and artifacts, and tweak hero stats. Backs up and checks your save on every change. Portable, about 5 MB.
